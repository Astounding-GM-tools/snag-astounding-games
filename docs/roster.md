# Roster: captains of Steamboat Snag Poker

The gallery is meant to grow to dozens of captains. Each tournament draws a
mixed line-up from it (see "Line-up" below).

Players only ever see a captain's **personality** (name, boat, a blurb) plus their own **notes**, typed into the captain
card and saved in their browser. The **playbook** below is internal: it lives
in `src/engine/ai.ts` and is written up here for us.

**Rights:** for the public release we use original captains, veiled homages,
or characters that are free worldwide (creator dead more than 70 years, no
active trademark). The right-hand column says which.

## Line-up

Each tournament draws, at random (seeded):

- **3 rascals**, **3 middling captains** and **1 sharp** at the start;
- **1 more sharp** arriving after the first captain is busted;
- **followers** (Morag) only alongside the captain they follow.

So a typical tournament starts with you plus seven rivals, with one or two more
arriving later. With an odd number of captains on the river, a random rival sits
each round out (never you, never twice running) and pays snag upkeep.

## Retired (borrowed characters, replaced for release)

Pete, Der Captain, Hans & Fritz, Jiggs, Maggie, Felix, Gaylord Ravenal and
Little Nemo were the prototype's captains. Their homages above keep the spirit;
older saved games still show their names.

## Ideas for the gallery

- **Krazy Kat:** throws away his *best* cards to see where it leads.
- **The Duke and the King** (Twain's river con men, free): natural bluffers.
- **Baron Munchausen** (free): the famous liar.
- **Happy Hooligan**, **the Yellow Kid** (both free).
- **Full-table boats:** the Walrus, the Carpenter and Max & Moritz on the
  *Cacophony*; a table of sharps on a grand showboat.

## Later: New Game Plus and Public Domain Day

After beating the tournament, a rotating table of characters; every 1 January,
newly public-domain characters may arrive (US dates; check EU/Norway before
release). End boss: the faceless captain of *The Shadow*, who can see your
hand (an original homage).

## Willie, the narrator

Willie is a young deckhand who helps you and narrates the tournament (his own
design: a round face and a deckhand's cap). He appears as a speech bubble or a
silent-film caption: explaining the first crossings, the occasional tip, and
Willie's reckoning on each captain card.
